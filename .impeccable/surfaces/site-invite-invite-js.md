## Scope

The formal invitation page (rebeccaandthomas.net, previewed at preview.html). Visitor mode: Experience, with Persuade's duty to make the RSVP obvious. Extends the incumbent save-the-date world; the brief pins fonts, palette, textures, chalk set, centred card-stack layout and section order. No concept tournament: the brief specifies the structure section by section.

## Audience, job, constraints

Guests on phones from a personalised email; most fly in from Australia/UK. Feel invited, learn date/venue/attire, RSVP by 17 Dec 2026, plan the weekend, leave a public note. RSVP never depends on animation.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An invitation that behaves like stationery on a candlelit table, not a wedding-template scroll of hero, cards and icons. The page is one continuous dinner table seen from above: the film opens it, paper cards are set down on it, chalk illustrations are drawn onto it.

OWN-WORLD: Beige cotton paper and fibre card textures, near-black chalk line drawings in their own grain, dark red #761d1d only for the guest's name, actions and the wax seal. Playfair Display italic for names and headings, Montserrat tracked uppercase for small labels. Soft offset shadows under cards like paper lifted off a tablecloth.

STORY: Arrive to the monogram being drawn; watch the table come alive under the scroll until the glass is taken away; read your name above the church; learn the weekend chapter by chapter; open the details; RSVP and see it sealed; read and leave notes; take the Malta guide with you; leave on the monogram and the countdown.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed overhead film frame, the silver tray and lace card centred, nothing overlaid but a small chalk-weight chevron at the bottom centre. On mobile, a 3:4 crop shifted left so the departing glass stays in shot.

FORM: Brief-pinned single-column card stack with one pinned horizontal chapter section; position: brief-specified, no seed key (concept-seed not run, structure pinned by the client).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment

The scroll-scrubbed film ending as the wine glass is drawn away, then the guest's own name appearing above the church as it is drawn in chalk.
